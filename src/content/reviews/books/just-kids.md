## Monday's Children

- I found very beatiful the way she describes her first time seeing a swan:
> A long curving neck rose from a dress of white plumage
>
- The view of the swan awoke a desire inside her to talk about its beauty. She even called its appearance a miracle. This yearning stayed with her even after the swan flew away;
- Patti was born during a great blizzard in 1946. Her mother went into heavy labor inside a taxi. She was born with pneumonia and her father kept her alive by holding her over the steam that came out of the bathtub;
- She had a sister, Linda; a brother, Todd. Theu lived under poor conditions, but even so, their parents tried to give them a good childhood;
- Her mother taught her to pray and it really bothered her having to repeat the same words instead of saying what really was on her heart and mind;
> It pleased me to imagine a presence above us, in continual motion, like liquid stars
>
- When she stopped repeating those same memorized words and started to make her own prayers, she saw what her imagination could do. As she stated, she would mouth long letters to God;
> My small torrent of words dissipared into an elaborate sense of expanding and receding. It was my entrance into the raciance of imagination.
>
- She was sick a lot, and she would spend those days bed ridden entertaining herself just using her imagination;
- Her first experience with the ideia of other worlds besides ours was throught prayers and religion; the second was with books, through her mother's influence;
    - She even hid her mother's books under her pillow to "absorb the meaning";
    - they bouth would sit on the couch and read together (each whith their own book);
- The books open a whole new world for her. She wanted to read every book ever written. And each book would make wanting to experience new things and meet new places;
    - But what she desired the most was to express herself and she started to do that with the support of her siblings, who would listen to her made up stories/scenarys, act on her plays and play what ever game she would made up;
    - [I have this feeling. I sometimes feel bad to think about all the music, books, movies that will come out after I'm dead that I won't even know the existence. And also I feel overwhelmed to think about all the works of art that have been released and that I haven't consumed]
- Stephanie was her friend, who was very sick. Patti admited that she was more interested on Stephanie's belongins than on her friendship;
    - On the last time that Patti saw Stephanie, she stole a pin while Stephanie was asleep and she felt immediate guilt. She couldn't sleep at night and swore that would take it back and beg for her forgiveness, wich she coundn't do because Stephanie had died the following day;
- Stephanie's mom gave her stuff to Patti, but she couldn't even look at them, covered with guilt;
- Patti tells us constantly how poor her childhood was;
- Robert was fascinated by that story;
> I was a bad girl trying to be good and he was a good boy trying to be bad.
>
